About the Studio

Profile

P. 04

"The best photographs happen when people forget the camera is there."

Aurelia Captures started in 2021 with a simple idea: show up prepared, stay out of the way, and let the moment lead. Whether it's a birthday party, a conference, a solo portrait session, or a collaboration with another brand, the approach stays the same.

Every shoot is planned around what the day actually needs, not a fixed package. That means real conversations before the camera comes out, and a gallery afterward that feels like the day, not a performance of it.
